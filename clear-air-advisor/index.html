    Advisor

    Load your city first, then ask anything about today's air — for example, "should my father take his morning walk now or later?"

    Answers are general guidance, not medical advice.

    Data sources

    Live weather, air composition and 48-hour pollution forecasts from OpenWeatherMap. CPCB-style index derived from PM2.5 using Central Pollution Control Board sub-index breakpoints.

    Method

    Recommendations combine statutory AQI guidance with emission-factor arithmetic. Quantified impacts are engineering estimates from published averages, stated so you can judge them, not lab measurements of your home.

    Limits

    General information for a hackathon demonstration, not medical, regulatory or legal advice. For health decisions during severe episodes, follow your doctor and official CPCB / SAFAR bulletins.

    Clear Air Advisor — built for the PromptWars GenAI Challenge, urban winter smog season, India.

    API setup

    Provider credentials are kept on the server and are never entered into this page. For local development, set GEMINI_API_KEY and OPENWEATHER_API_KEY in .env.local, then run scripts/dev.ps1 in PowerShell. For a deployment, add them as Secrets in the Vercel project settings.
